How Ratings Work

Each activity (Beach Walk, Dune Walk, etc.) for each hour is rated based on the forecast conditions (Feels Like Temp, Wind Speed, Rain Chance) compared to what's typical for that specific week in autumn at De Haan.

The typical weekly baseline is estimated based on average monthly conditions for October and November. This baseline helps determine if the forecast is better or worse than usual for the time of year.

The ratings aim to reflect realistic comfort levels for autumn coastal activities:

  • PERFECT: Exceptionally good conditions, rare for the season.
  • GOOD: Noticeably better than the weekly average.
  • PASSABLE: Around average for the week, or slightly worse but still manageable (within absolute safety/comfort limits).
  • BAD: Significantly worse than the weekly average, or exceeding absolute limits (e.g., wind > 25 km/h for Beach Walk).

Note: Absolute limits (like maximum wind for safety or minimum temperature for comfort) always override the comparison to the average. This is why 'Beach Walk' might be 'BAD' due to high wind, while 'Beach Stay' (assuming a shelter) might still be 'PASSABLE' at the same time.
